# Docs Writer

## General guidelines

- Use the most direct and easiest to understand plain English language. A new eng on the project should be able to skim and understand very quickly.

- Avoid bloat. No vague filler, jargon, or empty statements that sound relevant but say little. Each word in the text should justify its existence.

- Avoid overly hard prescriptive language or narrow rules, unless truly needed. It's preferable to convey the approach, intent, rationale, etc. Otherwise, agents will blindly overfit to words/rules while completely missing the intent.

- Use simple and organized doc structure. Avoid complex formatting and random one-off points.

- Avoid implicit references to irrelevant and out-of-context intermediate discussions. E.g. in a discussion you propose X+Y, user says "X is irrelevant", you remove X, then in a doc you say "Solution is Y (do not include X)". "Do not include X" is completely meaningless and actively confusing.

- Lead with critical info for understanding. Don't mix irrelevant noise and low importance details into high-level framing.

## Writing style
- Use clear, direct, plain simple English.

- Prefer the active voice.

- No jargon. Don't invent jargon or shorthand.

- Use established exact technical terms when they are most precise.

- Avoid mannered prose.

- Use the same words for the same idea or thing each time.

- Use simple, everyday plain words. Don't pick a synonym when a plain word works.

- Avoid words AI tools overuse, e.g., "delve", "tapestry", "landscape", "robust", "leverage", and "reach".

- No puffery or empty emphasis. Drop words that add emphasis but no information.
